Discover the entirety behind the emblem of strength and Cyber Security we uphold.

Exhibiting top notch credentials as a Cyber Safety companion, Cyber Alert embraces the responsibility of

  • Creating Cyber Awareness in the society
  • Offering Cyber Security services
  • Hosting elaborated Cyber Law content
  • Redefining Cyber Service paradigm with innovative endeavours

In today’s mesmerizing world of technological marvels, everything that glitters is not gold. As compelling as it is to exalt this glorious revolution, the responsibility to hire hope of safety is an undisputed necessity. Foiling unwelcome threats and cementing a fortress of trust, an indomitable creation should answer the qualm of insecurity. With the venue nothing but precisely suited, Cyber Alert arrives at the cyber scene. The call to create cyber awareness in the society is duly taken by this venture, concurrently gratifying the need for making available essential cyber law materials through the app.

Some of the path-breaking services brought to the fold by this application include:

Cyber Complaint Filing

Through this app the users can file a cyber complaint through the e-filing link which is provided with the disclaimer.

Cyber Complaint Tracker / Status

Cyber Materials

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Why should one need Cyber Alert?

    To create cyber awareness in people and overcome cyber crimes to safeguard the public from cyber crimes.

  • What is the motto of Cyber Alert?

    To create cyber awareness in people and in society. Users can have the access to read detailed cyber security & cyber law materials through this Mobile Application.

  • What services does Cyber Alert offer?

    Cyber Complaint Filing
    Cyber Complaint Tracker / Status
    Cyber Materials
    Cyber Police Stations
    Cyber Internships
    Cyber Legal Aid

  • What benefits does Cyber Alert treat users to?

    Easy and flexible complaint filing on cyber attacks through National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, finding cyber police stations, live talks with cyber experts, provision of cyber internships/workshops to the students and also providing materials on Cyber crimes and Cyber security.

  • How is Cyber Security significant for today’s fast-paced living?

    There is a huge demand for people trained in cybersecurity to help defend against the growing threat of Cyber Crime. It is a fast-paced, highly dynamic field with a huge number of specialties to choose from, including forensics, endpoint security, critical infrastructure, incident response, secure coding, and awareness and training.

    Users can find the app available on Google play Store.
